{% comment %} Scrim behind a surface in its bottom-sheet form: it dims the page and takes the clicks aimed at it, so a tap behind the sheet dismisses instead of hitting the link it landed on. (Only the clicks — the page below still scrolls and still takes Tab.) Render it *outside* the surface, in the same Alpine scope and with no stacking context between the two. Inside the surface it inherits its stacking context and paints over the panel's own background, and while `surface-rise` holds a transform it takes the panel as its containing block. Dismissal is the surface's own `@click.outside`, which every one of them carries and which a scrim click reaches by definition. Above 1100px the surface is an anchored popover and the scrim is `display: none`. Parameters: - show_expr (required) — the Alpine expression the surface's own ``x-show`` carries. {% endcomment %}
